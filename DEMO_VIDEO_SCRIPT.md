# 🎬 UrbanVista — 2 to 3 Minute Video Demo Pitch Script
**Target Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds  
**Tone:** Confident, professional, crisp, engineering-driven  
**Project Problem Statement:** Distributed Edge AI Road Safety & Municipal Transit Sensor Network  

---

## 📋 Recording Checklist Before Pressing Record:
1. **Screen Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080), browser at 100% zoom.
2. **Servers Running:** Both Frontend (`http://localhost:5173`) and Backend (`http://localhost:8000`) active.
3. **Demo Pitch Bar:** Notice the new **"🎬 DEMO PITCH MODE"** bar at the top of your dashboard. You can click stages 1 through 5 in order, or click **"🎙️ Teleprompter Speech"** to practice alongside the app!

---

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Script & On-Screen Actions

### 0:00 – 0:35 | Hook & Problem Statement
* **On Screen:** Start on the **City Overview (`/`)**. Click **"START LIVE SIMULATION"**, then on the top-right of the map click **"🔥 Heatmap"**.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Hello respected judges and evaluators! Every year, municipal corporations struggle with billions in road maintenance backlogs and fatal accidents caused by undetected potholes, road erosion, and damaged infrastructure. Traditional road survey vehicles are too rare and expensive to inspect an entire city continuously.*
  > 
  > *Welcome to **UrbanVista** — an intelligent municipal platform that transforms ordinary city public transit buses into distributed, mobile edge-sensing platforms that map city-wide road hazards in real time."*

---

### 0:35 – 1:10 | Onboard Edge AI Computer Vision
* **On Screen:** In the top header bar, click **"AI Vision Tester"**. Click **"Generate Sample Dashcam Frame"**, then click **"Run AI Detection"**. Hover over the red bounding box that appears.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Instead of streaming costly 4G HD video from thousands of buses to the cloud, each bus runs lightweight computer vision models directly at the edge.*
  > 
  > *Watch as our vision pipeline inspects this incoming camera feed. Within milliseconds, it detects a critical road crater, draws localized bounding boxes with 94% confidence, and transmits only a lightweight 2-kilobyte telemetry packet to the municipal command center — saving 99.5% of cellular bandwidth."*

---

### 1:10 – 1:45 | Spatial Deduplication & Closed-Loop Contractor SLA
* **On Screen:** Close modal. Navigate to **Incidents** (`/incidents`). Click **"⬢ Spatial Deduplicated Assets"**. Then click on any incident (e.g. `INC-26-001`). Scroll down to the **"Before & After AI Re-Scan Verification"** panel and click **"Simulate Bus Re-Scan: Compliant Patch"**. Also click the header button **"Export Work Order"** to show the downloaded CSV.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"In real life, if 20 buses pass the same pothole on MG Road, they would flood municipal officers with duplicate tickets. Our spatial clustering engine automatically groups nearby detections within 75 meters into a single unique Road Asset ID with multi-bus verification.*
  > 
  > *Even better, we close the contractor accountability loop. When a contractor claims a pothole is fixed, the next bus automatically re-scans the coordinates. Our AI compares the Before and After frames to verify IRC road surface standards before releasing contractor payments!"*

---

### 1:45 – 2:15 | Onboard Bus Driver Safety HUD
* **On Screen:** In the left sidebar, click **"Driver HUD"** (`/driver-hud`). Point your cursor at the digital speedometer and the central **Proximity Hazard Radar Warning**.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"UrbanVista doesn't just inform administrators — it actively protects commuters and bus drivers right on the road.*
  > 
  > *Here is our **Onboard Driver Cockpit HUD**. As the vehicle travels its route, our proximity radar calculates upcoming road hazards in real time, alerting the driver 65 meters before a critical pothole with safe speed limits and lane guidance to prevent transit damage and accidents."*

---

### 2:15 – 2:40 | Citizen Transparency Portal & Winning Conclusion
* **On Screen:** In the sidebar, click **"Citizen Portal"** (`/citizen-portal`). Type `"MG Road"`, point to the **Road Quality Index (RQI)** dial (e.g. 82/100), and click **"Submit Civic Grievance"** to show the instant bus fleet match badge.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Finally, our Citizen Portal gives the public total transparency with live Road Quality Index scores for every municipal ward. When a citizen reports a hazard, our system verifies whether a transit bus has already flagged it, providing instant civic accountability.*
  > 
  > *UrbanVista creates a complete closed loop between public transit sensors, municipal governance, and citizen safety for smarter, resilient cities. Thank you!"*

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for Maximum Marks:
1. **Speak with Energy:** Pacing is everything. Don't rush; speak clearly and pause for 1 second after switching views.
2. **Smooth Mouse Movements:** Move your cursor deliberately to what you are talking about.
3. **If You Make a Mistake:** Don't restart the whole recording; just pause for 3 seconds and repeat the sentence — you can easily trim the pause in any video editor!
